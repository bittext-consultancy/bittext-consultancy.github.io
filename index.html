Services

AI and software advisory

Independent advice for software teams adopting AI or improving how they build software, from early assessment through to a working implementation.

  • AI strategy. Identifying where AI fits a product, estimating cost, effort and risk, and choosing between off-the-shelf models, fine-tuning and custom builds.
  • Implementation. Designing and building AI features such as retrieval, agents and automated workflows, along with the evaluation and monitoring needed to run them in production.
  • Engineering practice reviews. Reviewing architecture, code quality, testing, security and release processes against current industry practice, with a prioritised plan of improvements.
  • AI safety and security. Guardrails, red-teaming and data protection that keep AI systems reliable and resistant to misuse such as prompt injection.

Technical interviews

Structured technical interviews for software engineering roles, with written, rubric-based feedback on each candidate.

AI training data

Training and evaluation data for AI models: writing expert responses, reviewing model outputs and rating them against quality guidelines.

Clients

  • Technical hiring platforms used by large technology employers to interview and assess engineering candidates.
  • AI training data companies that supply training and evaluation data to leading AI labs.
