Bittext Consultancy Private Limited is a consultancy based in India. We advise software companies on adopting AI and modern engineering practice, conduct technical interviews for engineering hiring, and produce training and evaluation data for AI models.

Services

We work with software companies in three areas.

AI and software advisory

Independent advice for software teams adopting AI or improving how they build software, from early assessment through to a working implementation.

  • AI strategyIdentifying where AI fits a product, estimating cost, effort and risk, and choosing between off-the-shelf models, fine-tuning and custom builds.
  • ImplementationDesigning and building AI features such as retrieval, agents and automated workflows, along with the evaluation and monitoring needed to run them in production.
  • Engineering practice reviewsReviewing architecture, code quality, testing, security and release processes against current industry practice, with a prioritised plan of improvements.
  • AI safety and securityGuardrails, red-teaming and data protection that keep AI systems reliable and resistant to misuse such as prompt injection.

Technical interviews

Structured technical interviews for software engineering roles, with written, rubric-based feedback on each candidate.

AI training data

Training and evaluation data for AI models: writing expert responses, reviewing model outputs and rating them against quality guidelines.
